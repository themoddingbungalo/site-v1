**Made by Camora0**

From the maker of Wasteland Reborn in collaboration with the author of LoreRim comes LoreOut - The Modern Roleplaying Modlist. LoreOut is a modern, immersive, lore-focused reimagining of **Fallout 4** with over 800 mods. Every aspect of the game has been shaped to enhance the gameplay, visuals, roleplayability and much more. This is an auto-installing Wabbajack modlist filled with some of the best mods the community has to offer. Dive in and don't give up!

For full installation instructions, checkout the [Read Me](/lists/loreout/read-me/)

## Modern Gunplay, Animations & Roleplaying

LoreOut is built off of Lunar Fallout Overhaul Deluxe and You Are Exceptional for a unique roleplaying experience. Skills are added back to the game, perks matter, weapons are rebalanced. Weapons have been completely modernized while staying lore-friendly. No more ugly pipe weapons with 2015 animations. No more shooting enemies 50 times in the face while they shrug it off. Gunplay should feel smooth, realistic and deadly. Aiming for the weak spots of enemies will kill them fast but be careful, as they can do the same to you.

## Visuals, Atmosphere & World

NAC X weathers, Reactor ENB and a plethora of landscape, water, foliage and other overhauls bring the game's visuals to modern standards. Everything that can be modernized has been modernized, from small clutter to weapons to buildings. NPCs have also been re-imagined based on concept art or with care to the NPC's personality and style. Finally, every tool has been utilized to ensure that the game's LODs and precombines are done the right way, ensuring both visual fidelity and consistency anywhere in the Commonwealth.

## New Armors, Weapons & Collectibles

You will not run out of things to collect in LoreOut. Items have been placed all around the world or injected into leveled lists to make sure that every enemy can have a chance for some unique loot. Tons of new armor sets, clothing sets and weapons dot the landscape and raider inventories. Sticking to lore, you won't find modern real-life weapons. Instead, LoreOut adds weapons that were confirmed to exist in previous games or mentioned in canon. Along your travels, you will also find unique items that give you special bonuses to your stats and abilities. Make sure to check every container!

## Quests, Lands & Settlements

LoreOut adds plenty of new quests and content to extend your playtime, with classic and highly acclaimed mods such as Fourville, America Rising 2, Tales of the Commonwealth and more. New companions such as Molly will join you on your travels as you experience these mods in stunning detail. Every mod has been checked for consistency, balance, bugfixes and patches. The settlement system has been simplified with the Rebuild Collection AIO, letting you repair settlements so that you can personally decorate and populate them to your liking. If you somehow manage to do all the content in LoreOut, don't worry - LoreOut is designed for replayability so that no two playthroughs are the same.
